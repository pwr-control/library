# Documentation

Technical notes documenting the components of this library. Each subfolder holds the LaTeX source
and the compiled PDF, and — where the note describes a Simscape component — a copy of the `.ssc`
file it documents, plus the MATLAB, Python, data and figure files used to produce it.

The folder was tidied up by `organize_docs.py`, which grouped the files that used to sit flat in
the root into one folder per note, following the layout of `div_load_model`. It never deletes, it
only moves, and it records every move in `.organize_manifest.json` so the operation can be undone
(`--apply`, `--undo`, `--plan`; it runs dry by default). The short `README.md` files inside the
subfolders were generated by that script and are still mostly empty — this file is the index that
replaces them.

---

## Semiconductor device models

### `igbt_dyn`
*Modelli Simscape per la dinamica di commutazione: `diode_rr` e `igbt_dyn`* (Italian, rev. 1.1).
Documents the two components used to study IGBT cell turn-on and turn-off: overvoltage at turn
off, free-wheeling diode reverse recovery, ringing against the loop stray inductance, and hence
snubber sizing. Both are written fully continuous, with no `if/else` and no events: the piecewise
nonlinearities are replaced by smooth regularizations, which reduces zero crossings for
variable-step solvers. No losses, no temperature dependence. Contains `ssc/igbt_dyn.ssc`.

### `diode_rr`
Charge-controlled diode (Lauritzen-Ma 1991) for switching-transient studies: Irr, trr and recovery
softness emerge from the circuit di/dt and the two charge parameters instead of being imposed.
Documented inside the `igbt_dyn` note; this folder holds `ssc/diode_rr.ssc` only.

### `mos_dyn`
*Modello Simscape `mos_dyn` — Dinamica di commutazione di un MOSFET di potenza* (Italian,
rev. 1.0). The MOSFET counterpart of `igbt_dyn`: real dv/dt and di/dt with the Miller plateau and
its dependence on gate resistance and drive voltage, two-quadrant channel conduction,
charge-controlled body-diode recovery, common-source inductance feedback on the gate loop and an
optional avalanche clamp. Does not cover losses, thermal effects, threshold degradation or short
circuit; for SiC the transfer characteristic is less quadratic, so the transconductance parameter
must be fitted near rated current. Contains `ssc/mos_dyn.ssc`.

### `igbt_simple`
*A Simplified Simscape IGBT Model with Single-Point Switching Energies and Thermal Ports.*
Conduction as fixed voltage drops, switching losses as three single-point energies given at one
reference point and scaled with commutated current and blocking voltage, dissipated on one or two
thermal ports. Covers the sampling scheme that recovers the commutated quantities, the
energy-to-power conversion, datasheet parameter extraction and the validity limits, including the
local detection of the diode recovery event in the two-port version. Defaults: Mitsubishi
CM1200DW-34T. Contains `ssc/igbt_simple_2th.ssc`.

### `mos_simple_2th`
*`mos_simple_2th`: simplified power MOSFET loss model with separate thermal ports* (Rev. 00).
The MOSFET counterpart of `igbt_simple_2th` — same interface, same energy scaling, same
energy-to-power conversion — with the differences imposed by the device: a bidirectional resistive
channel with third-quadrant conduction, a body diode carrying current only during the dead time,
zero-voltage turn-on and a diode on the same chip as the channel. Contains
`ssc/mos_simple_2th.ssc`.

### `mos_loss`
Power MOSFET loss model (channel plus intrinsic body diode) for loss estimation and nominal
thermal dimensioning, with temperature-dependent on-state resistance and forward drop, switching
energies scaled with voltage, current and junction temperature, and two thermal ports. Switching
energies are accumulated at the events and released through a first-order filter, so the energy is
conserved. No note; the folder holds `ssc/mos_loss.ssc` only.

### `thyristor_rr`
*A Simscape Language Thyristor Model with Reverse Recovery — Lumped-Charge (Lauritzen-Ma) Dynamics
with Latching Logic and a Physical tq Constraint.* Simscape Electrical has no thyristor block with
junction charge dynamics: the piecewise-linear block treats reverse recovery only as a loss term
and the Specialized Power Systems detailed thyristor models tq without the recovery waveform. The
note combines the piecewise-linear static characteristic with the lumped-charge model so the
terminal current reproduces the recovery peak, the stored charge and the tail; tq is reproduced
through a residual-charge re-trigger condition plus an explicit timer. Parameters are extracted in
full for the ABB 5STP 26N6500 and validated in a six-pulse rectifier, matching the datasheet
recovered charge within 5 %. Contains `ssc/thyristor_rr.ssc`.

### `tvs_clamp`
Bidirectional TVS stack (N series × M parallel) for IGBT active clamping: symmetric avalanche with
an algebraic smooth knee, constant dynamic resistance, sub-breakdown leakage and lumped
capacitance; device temperature is a parameter, no self-heating. Defaults: 1.5KE440CA. Documented
by the `active_clamping_5sna1000g650300` note; this folder holds `ssc/tvs_clamp.ssc` only.

## Protection and clamping

### `active_clamping_5sna1000g650300`
*Active Clamping of High-Voltage IGBTs: Theory, Design for a 6.5 kV / 1000 A Module and a Simscape
TVS Model.* Active clamping feeds the collector-emitter voltage back to the gate through a TVS
stack: above the stack breakdown the device is driven into linear conduction and absorbs the
inductive commutation energy at a limited voltage. Reviews the principle and its variants, then
develops the full design procedure for the ABB HiPak 5SNA 1000G650300 — tolerance, temperature and
energy budgets, coordination with an external MOV clamp — and documents the `tvs_clamp` component
used to verify it together with `igbt_dyn`.

### `crowbar_bidir`
*Bidirectional Self-Triggered Thyristor Crowbar — Bypass Protection of a Low-Impedance Element in
the 3 kV / 3600 A Bench* (Rev. 00). An element that is structurally a short circuit sits in the
current path of an inductive test bench; if it opens, the loop inductance drives its voltage up
without limit. The note describes a fully passive bidirectional crowbar in parallel with it — two
antiparallel thyristors, each fired by a floating break-over network, shorting the element within
microseconds in either polarity — with the coordination rules for snubber capacitor, MOV, series
reactor, firing level and thermal duty, a worked example and a test procedure. Contains
`ssc/crowbar_bidir.ssc`.

### `snubber_design`
*Snubber Design Guidelines for a Half-Bridge Leg Based on the Infineon FZ2400R33HE4 IHM-B Module.*
Defines the voltage budget, quantifies the commutation loop inductance, compares the practical
snubber topologies, gives closed-form sizing equations and works through a numerical example
including loss estimation, component selection and test verification, closing with how to rescale
the result to a 1700 V device of the same current class. Note only, no component.

## Magnetics

### `twolimb_inductor_with_saturation`
*Two-Limb Inductor with Saturation* (Rev. 00). A single-phase reactor with one winding on each
limb of a common core, i.e. the two-winding counterpart of the single-phase saturable inductor:
same parameters and same saturation table, with half the inductance and half the resistance in
each conductor. Typical uses are DC-link reactors split between the rails, thyristor-rectifier
output reactors split in both poles, and single-phase AC reactors wound on two limbs. Covers the
magnetic model, the saturation characteristic, the equivalence with the single-phase block, the
implementation, an equation-level verification and the limitations. Contains
`ssc/twolimb_inductor_with_saturation_1.ssc`.

### `singlephase_inductor_with_saturation`
Series inductor with saturable main inductance, non-saturable leakage and winding resistance. The
saturation table is the differential inductance dψ/di normalized to the rated inductance, against
the current normalized to the peak base current; setting the base current to infinity disables
saturation. The flux linkage is integrated from u − R·i, so its zero initial value is consistent
only with zero initial current. No note; the folder holds the `.ssc` with its revision history.

### `scott_t`
*The Scott-T Transformer: Three-Phase to Two-Phase Conversion and its Simscape Implementation.*
Derives the connection rule and the √3/2 turns ratio of the teaser winding, gives the current
relations and the behaviour in symmetrical components under unbalanced two-phase load, and
documents the equations, per-unit parameterization and sign conventions of the component.
Contains `ssc/scott_t.ssc`.

## Loads and thermal

### `div_load_model`
*DTT DIV in-vessel coil load model for disruption studies — Simscape implementation,
identification on the ENEA specification curves and validation* (Rev. 00). Models the three DTT
divertor in-vessel coils as seen from the power-supply terminals during a plasma disruption, so
the complete converter, filter, crowbar and protection-coil circuit can be simulated. Each coil is
a Thevenin equivalent: the plasma-induced open-circuit e.m.f. in series with coil resistance, self
inductance and two eddy-current loops identified on the frequency-dependent impedance of the
specification. The identification shows that the specified crowbar current is not the response of
the specified circuit to the specified e.m.f., and is reproduced only with a 1.84 gain; both worst
cases ship as parameter sets. This is the most complete package of the folder — components under
`ssc/+dtt/`, parameter and validation scripts under `matlab/`, the identification and figure
scripts under `py/`, the digitized curves and fit results under `data/`, the figures under `fig/`,
and a hand-written README with a quick start.

### `hs778746_thermal`
*Thermal Impedance Estimate of the Clamp Heatsink 778746 — PADA profile "G", 225 × 91 mm section,
L = 250 mm, forced air cooling* (Rev. 0). Estimates the heatsink-to-ambient thermal resistance and
the transient thermal impedance of the clamp heatsink used with a 100 mm press-pack thyristor,
with two independent models: an analytical chain (channel convection, fin analysis, spreading
resistance) and a 3-D finite-difference model that also gives the step response. Result: about
0.080 K/W at 140 m³/h, falling to 0.068 K/W at 280 m³/h, with a four-cell Foster network for
transients. Scripts under `py/`.
